# 🌱 Seed the Vault — already in the role

> You're not new; the vault is. Spend one or two sessions moving what's in your head (and your old notes) into the places the co-pilot reads. After that the daily pass keeps it current. Tick as you go.

## 1. People (the highest-value hour)
- [ ] Create a `02-People/` page for everyone you talk to regularly — manager, reports, closest peers, key stakeholders (use `99-Templates/Person.md`; fill the `Email:` line so meetings match)
- [ ] On each page, write the **📦 Open items** as they stand today: what you owe them, what they owe you, what you keep meaning to raise
- [ ] Note each person's cadence with you and anything you'd want a new you to know about working with them

## 2. Your own commitments
- [ ] Dump every open commitment into [[My Tasks]] under its disposition: implement myself / raise with team / raise with managers (one ask = one checkbox)
- [ ] Park what you're honestly not doing this quarter under ❄️ Parked, with a date
- [ ] Put standing questions nobody has answered yet into [[Open Questions]]

## 3. What you already know
> Don't type what the org already wrote down. In the vault, ask Claude: *"seed [[Team Overview]] from connected sources"* — it reads the wiki / chat / tracker read-only, drafts the page with citations, and puts what it couldn't find into [[Open Questions]]. Then correct it; that's faster than writing from scratch.
- [ ] [[Team Overview]]: mission, roster, who owns what, the current focus
- [ ] [[Team Processes]] and [[How We Work]]: the rituals, how work gets planned, reviewed and shipped — as they *actually* run
- [ ] [[{{PRODUCT_NAME}}]]: the shape of the thing you work on, in a page you'd hand your replacement
- [ ] One `05-Knowledge/` page per system, concept or tool you explain more than twice a year
- [ ] [[Glossary]]: the acronyms and codenames newcomers trip over

## 4. In-flight work
- [ ] One `04-My-Team/Projects/` page per active project (`99-Templates/Project.md`): why, scope, milestones, decisions so far
- [ ] Add the three or four past decisions people still argue about to the relevant page's decisions log

## 5. Your voice
- [ ] Build [[Voice Profile]] + [[Exemplars]] from your own past messages ([[07-Voice/README]]) so drafts sound like you

## 6. Start the loop
- [ ] Read [[How to Use This Vault]] once — the moves for mornings, 1-1s, to-dos and end of day
- [ ] Write today's daily note; capture one `- raise <topic> with [[Person]]`
- [ ] Before your next 1-1, open that person's page and check the 📦 Open items are complete — tomorrow's prep is built from them
- [ ] Run the daily pass once by hand and read the brief it produces
