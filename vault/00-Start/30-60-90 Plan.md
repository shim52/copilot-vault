# 🎯 30-60-90 Day Plan — {{ROLE}}

> **For when you're new to the role** (already in it? use [[Seed the Vault]] instead). Agree this with [[{{MANAGER_NAME}}]] in week one and revisit it at each milestone. Keep items concrete and checkable; move answered questions to [[Open Questions]] → Resolved. Works for any role: the arc is **learn → contribute → own**.

## 🗓️ First 30 days — LEARN
- [ ] Meet the people I'll work with most; create a `02-People/` page for each (manager, team, closest partners)
- [ ] Understand [[{{PRODUCT_NAME}}]]: what it does, for whom, how it's built or delivered
- [ ] Learn [[How We Work]] — cadence, tools, how work is planned, reviewed and shipped
- [ ] Sit in on the existing rituals without changing them
- [ ] Ship something small end to end to learn the path
- [ ] Collect what I don't understand and the pains I hear into [[Open Questions]]

## 🗓️ Days 30-60 — CONTRIBUTE
- [ ] Take on a meaningful piece of work and deliver it
- [ ] Give my first real feedback on how things work (in a 1-1, not a broadcast)
- [ ] Settle my cadences: 1-1s, the `📦 Open items` habit, daily capture → distill
- [ ] Document one thing nobody had written down yet (→ `05-Knowledge/` or `01-Org/`)

## 🗓️ Days 60-90 — OWN
- [ ] Own an area, project or outcome and be the person people come to for it
- [ ] Propose one improvement with evidence, and get a decision on it
- [ ] Review the first quarter with [[{{MANAGER_NAME}}]]: what worked, what to change, what's next
